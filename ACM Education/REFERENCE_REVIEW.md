## Shared patterns incorporated

These recur across the readable portfolios; they are not universal requirements.

- A prominent name and concise personal/technical introduction.
- An about section with university and degree context.
- Selected work described in concrete terms, with technologies or topic tags.
- An education, experience, leadership, or community section.
- An organized skills/toolkit presentation.
- Easy access to contact information, verified social links, and a résumé.
- A connection to the UTD webring where appropriate.
- Distinctive visual identity and interactive details.

## Original direction

Graphite backgrounds, mint accents, fine rules, small monospace labels, generous
spacing, and an original interactive orbital wireframe give Vishruth's page a
futuristic character. Original document and robot illustrations support the two
résumé projects without pretending to be screenshots. The layout and copy were
written for Vishruth; no peer's biography, achievements, photographs, or source
code were copied.
